About King's Plumbing

Calgary's trusted plumbing and HVAC experts since 2005.

These Guys Are Great Plumbers

Founded in 2005, King's Plumbing & HVAC has been serving Calgary homeowners and businesses with professional, prompt, and friendly service. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a trusted name in the Calgary plumbing industry.

Our mission is simple: provide premium quality service at competitive rates. We believe in doing the job right the first time, and standing behind our work with integrity and professionalism.

Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, we're here when you need us most. From emergency repairs to planned installations, we bring the same level of dedication and expertise to every job.
