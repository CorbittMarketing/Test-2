Plumbing & HVAC Tips

Expert advice to keep your systems running smoothly and avoid costly repairs.

🚿 Hot Water Tank Inspection Tips

Annual Maintenance Checklist:

  • Check for leaks around the tank base and connections
  • Test the temperature-pressure relief (TPR) valve
  • Drain and flush the tank to remove sediment buildup
  • Inspect the anode rod every 2-3 years
  • Check thermostat settings (recommended: 120°F)
⚠️ Warning: If you notice rust-colored water or rumbling noises, call a professional immediately. These are signs of tank failure.

🔥 Furnace Maintenance Tips

Before Winter Season:

  • Replace or clean furnace filters every 1-3 months
  • Check and clean blower motor and fan blades
  • Inspect ductwork for leaks or blockages
  • Test thermostat accuracy
  • Schedule professional inspection annually

Signs You Need Service:

  • Uneven heating throughout your home
  • Unusual noises (banging, squealing, rattling)
  • Higher than normal energy bills
  • Frequent cycling on and off
  • Yellow burner flame (should be blue)
💡 Tip: Annual furnace maintenance can reduce energy costs by up to 30% and extend equipment life.

💧 Drain Care & Prevention

Do's:

  • Use drain strainers to catch hair and debris
  • Run hot water after using the sink
  • Clean pop-up stoppers regularly
  • Schedule annual professional drain cleaning

Don'ts:

  • Never pour grease or oil down drains
  • Avoid chemical drain cleaners (damage pipes)
  • Don't flush wipes, paper towels, or feminine products
  • Don't ignore slow drains — they get worse

❄️ Air Conditioner Tips

Pre-Season Checklist:

  • Clean or replace air filters
  • Clear debris from outdoor unit (2 feet clearance)
  • Check refrigerant lines for damage
  • Test system before hot weather hits
  • Schedule professional tune-up

Energy Saving Tips:

  • Use programmable thermostats
  • Keep blinds closed during peak sun hours
  • Use ceiling fans to circulate cool air
  • Seal windows and doors to prevent cool air escape

🆘 Emergency Shut-Off Procedures

Know Where Your Shut-Offs Are:

  • Main Water Valve: Usually near front foundation wall or where main line enters house
  • Gas Shut-Off: Near gas meter — requires wrench to turn
  • Electrical Panel: Know which breaker controls your water heater and furnace
🚨 Emergency? If you smell gas, leave immediately and call 911. For water emergencies, shut off main valve and call us at 403-555-0123 — available 24/7!
