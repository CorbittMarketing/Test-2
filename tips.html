Plumbing & HVAC Tips

Expert advice to keep your systems running smoothly and avoid costly repairs.

🚿 Hot Water Tank Inspection

Annual Maintenance Checklist:

  • Check for leaks around tank base and connections
  • Test temperature-pressure relief (TPR) valve
  • Drain and flush tank to remove sediment
  • Inspect anode rod every 2-3 years
  • Check thermostat settings (120°F recommended)
⚠️ Warning: Rust-colored water or rumbling noises indicate tank failure. Call immediately.

🔥 Furnace Maintenance

Before Winter:

  • Replace filters every 1-3 months
  • Clean blower motor and fan blades
  • Inspect ductwork for leaks
  • Test thermostat accuracy
  • Schedule annual professional inspection
💡 Tip: Annual maintenance reduces energy costs by up to 30%.

🚽 Toilet Care

Why TOTO:

  • Most reliable toilet on the market
  • Cheaper replacement parts
  • Parts last longer than competitors
  • Better flush technology

Common Issues:

  • Running toilet — usually flapper
  • Weak flush — clean bowl or adjust water level
  • Leaking base — wax ring replacement

💧 Drain Care

Do:

  • Use drain strainers
  • Run hot water after use
  • Schedule annual professional cleaning

Don't:

  • Pour grease down drains
  • Use chemical drain cleaners
  • Flush wipes or paper towels
💡 Trenchless Repair: We fix sewer lines with ZERO digging.

❄️ Air Conditioner

Pre-Season:

  • Clean or replace filters
  • Clear debris from outdoor unit
  • Check refrigerant lines
  • Test before hot weather

Energy Saving:

  • Use programmable thermostats
  • Keep blinds closed during peak sun
  • Use ceiling fans

🆘 Emergency Shut-Offs

Know where these are:

  • Main Water: Near front foundation wall
  • Gas: Near meter (requires wrench)
  • Electrical: Breaker panel location
